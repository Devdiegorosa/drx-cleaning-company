What Is Carpet Cleaning?

More Than a Surface Clean — Deep Extraction

Regular vacuuming removes surface debris, but over time your carpet accumulates layers of embedded dirt, dust mites, allergens, bacteria, and odor-causing residues that a vacuum cannot reach. Florida's warm, humid climate accelerates this buildup significantly.

DRX Cleaning uses hot-water extraction (HWE) — the cleaning method recommended by carpet manufacturers including Shaw, Mohawk, and Stainmaster. Our professional-grade truck-mounted equipment heats water to high temperatures, injects it deep into carpet fibers at high pressure, then immediately extracts the contaminated water along with all dissolved dirt, bacteria, and allergens.

The result is a carpet that is genuinely clean at the fiber level — not just surface-treated. Customers consistently report that their carpets look brighter, feel softer, and smell fresher than they have in years. Most importantly, allergen and bacteria counts drop dramatically, making your indoor environment healthier for your entire family.

We serve single rooms, whole homes, apartments, rental properties, and commercial spaces throughout Orlando and Central Florida. Whether you need routine maintenance cleaning, post-party deep cleaning, or move-in/move-out preparation, DRX delivers consistent, professional results backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.

Why Clean Your Carpets

6 Benefits of Professional Carpet Cleaning

A clean carpet is more than aesthetics — it's a healthier, longer-lasting, and more welcoming home environment.

Healthier Indoor Air

Carpets trap airborne pollutants including pollen, pet dander, mold spores, and dust mites. Professional extraction removes these at the source, dramatically improving the air your family breathes.

Complete Stain Removal

Red wine, coffee, pet urine, juice, and mud stains that seem permanent respond to our professional enzyme pre-treatment and high-pressure extraction. Most stains are fully eliminated — not just lightened.

Extended Carpet Life

Abrasive soil particles embedded in carpet fibers cut and fray fiber strands over time. Regular professional cleaning removes these particles and can double the effective life of your carpet.

Odor Elimination

Pet accidents, food spills, and general foot traffic create persistent odors that air fresheners only mask. Our enzyme-based treatments and hot-water extraction eliminate odors at the molecular level.

Bacteria & Allergen Reduction

Studies show carpets can harbor up to 4,000 times more bacteria than a toilet seat. Our high-temperature extraction eliminates bacteria, mold spores, and allergens that cause respiratory issues.

Restored Appearance

Professionally cleaned carpets look brighter, feel softer, and stand up straighter. Whether you are selling your home or simply refreshing your space, clean carpets make a dramatic visual difference.

Our Method

How DRX Cleans Your Carpet

Our 6-step process is designed to achieve the deepest possible clean while being completely safe for your carpet, your family, and the environment.

Book a Cleaning
1
Pre-Inspection & Assessment

We walk your space to assess carpet type, fiber material, traffic patterns, and identify problem areas including stains, high-wear zones, and any areas requiring special treatment.

2
Dry Soil Removal (HEPA Vacuuming)

Professional HEPA vacuuming removes dry soil, pet hair, and loose debris before wet cleaning — a critical step most DIY cleaners skip that dramatically improves final results.

3
Enzyme Pre-Treatment

Eco-safe enzyme solutions are applied to stained areas and heavy-traffic zones. These agents break down protein-based stains (pet urine, food, blood) at the molecular level before extraction begins.

4
Hot-Water Extraction

Our truck-mounted equipment delivers hot water under high pressure into carpet fibers, then immediately extracts it along with dissolved dirt, bacteria, allergens, and cleaning solution residue.

5
Post-Treatment & Spot Check

We address any remaining spots with targeted treatment, groom carpet fibers for even drying, and apply deodorizer to leave your home smelling fresh and clean.

6
Final Inspection with You

We walk you through the results before we leave. If any area does not meet your expectations, we address it on the spot — no arguments, no extra charges, no return visits needed.
