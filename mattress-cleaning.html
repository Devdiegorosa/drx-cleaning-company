The Surface You Sleep On

The Surface You Sleep On Is It Really Clean?

The average person spends 8 hours per night on their mattress — shedding skin cells, perspiring, and breathing allergens trapped inside the mattress for months. A new mattress can contain millions of dust mites within its first year of use, and no mattress cover fully prevents this buildup.

DRX Cleaning uses hot-water extraction combined with UV-C light sanitization to deep clean and disinfect your mattress at the surface and fiber level. Our process removes the visible stains and the invisible threats — dust mites, their waste products, mold spores, bacteria, and accumulated allergens.

Florida's year-round warmth and high humidity create an ideal environment for dust mite and mold growth inside mattresses. This is especially problematic for children, elderly individuals, and anyone with asthma or allergies. Regular professional mattress cleaning dramatically reduces these biological triggers.

Our technicians clean all mattress types including memory foam, innerspring, hybrid, latex, and pillow-top mattresses. We select appropriate cleaning methods for each material to ensure thorough sanitization without moisture damage. Most mattresses are ready to sleep on within 3–5 hours of cleaning.

Why It Matters

Benefits of Professional Mattress Cleaning

Dust Mite Elimination

A typical mattress can contain up to 2 million dust mites. Our UV sanitization and hot-water extraction eliminate dust mites and their waste products — one of the most common indoor allergen triggers.

Allergen Reduction

Dust mite waste, pet dander, mold spores, and pollen trapped in your mattress are extracted during cleaning. Most allergy and asthma sufferers report noticeable improvement in symptoms within days.

Stain & Odor Removal

Sweat, urine (including children's accidents), blood, and other organic stains respond to our enzyme pre-treatment and extraction. Both the stain and its associated odor are fully eliminated.

Bacteria Elimination

Studies show mattresses harbor significant bacterial populations. Our high-temperature extraction and UV-C sanitization kill bacteria that cause odors and can affect health over time.

Better Sleep Quality

A clean, allergen-free sleep surface leads to fewer nighttime allergy symptoms, better breathing, and measurably improved sleep quality. Customers consistently report sleeping better after professional cleaning.

Extended Mattress Life

Accumulated oils, sweat, and debris break down mattress materials over time. Regular professional cleaning removes these compounds and can significantly extend your mattress's useful life.

Our Method

How DRX Gets It Done

Every step of our process is designed to maximize results while being completely safe for your home, your family, and your property.

Book a Cleaning
1

Pre-Cleaning Inspection

We assess the mattress type, fabric, and condition to select the appropriate cleaning approach. We identify all stained areas, note any specific concerns, and discuss your expectations.

2

HEPA Pre-Vacuuming

Thorough HEPA vacuuming removes surface dust, skin cells, pet hair, and dry debris — especially important before any wet cleaning to prevent these particles from being pushed deeper into the mattress.

3

Enzyme Pre-Treatment for Stains

Stained areas receive targeted enzyme solution application. These bio-active agents break down protein-based stains (urine, blood, sweat) at the molecular level before extraction begins.

4

Hot-Water Extraction Cleaning

Low-moisture hot-water extraction cleans the mattress surface and upper fiber layers, removing dissolved contaminants, allergens, bacteria, and cleaning solution residue without over-saturating the mattress core.

5

UV-C Light Sanitization

We apply UV-C light treatment across the entire mattress surface. UV-C light has been clinically proven to eliminate bacteria, mold spores, and dust mites that extraction may not reach.

6

Deodorizing & Final Dry

A fabric-safe deodorizer is applied, and we encourage ventilation to speed drying. Most mattresses are ready to sleep on within 3–5 hours.
