What's Living In Your Carpet?

Carpet and upholstery trap far more than visible dirt. Dust mites, bacteria, mold spores, and allergens build up deep in the fibers — invisible to the eye, but very real for anyone with allergies, asthma, or a compromised immune system.

DRX deep cleaning combines hospital-grade hot-water extraction with a UV-C light sanitization pass across the entire treated surface. The extraction lifts embedded dirt and contaminants, while UV-C light disinfects at the microbial level — killing bacteria and mold spores that extraction alone doesn't reach.

Florida's heat and humidity accelerate bacterial growth and mold spore activity year-round, making periodic deep cleaning more important here than in drier climates. It's the difference between a carpet that looks clean and one that actually is.

We inspect carpet and upholstery condition, identify high-traffic and high-risk areas, and discuss any specific health or allergy concerns.
