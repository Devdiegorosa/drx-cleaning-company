Why DIY Doesn't Work

Some Stains Need More Than a Store Product

Store-bought spot removers and carpet shampoos only treat the surface fibers. Wine, urine, coffee, and grease penetrate deep into carpet backing, upholstery padding, and mattress foam — where consumer products simply can't reach.

DRX identifies the stain type first, then applies the right pre-treatment for that specific stain — enzyme-based solutions for organic stains like urine and blood, solvent-based treatments for oil and grease, and specialized agents for dye-based stains like wine and ink. Using the wrong product on the wrong stain can set it permanently, which is why professional diagnosis matters.

After pre-treatment, hot-water extraction lifts the loosened stain and residue completely — leaving no sticky residue behind to attract new dirt, unlike many over-the-counter cleaners.

Why It Matters

Benefits of Professional Stain Removal

Wine & Juice Removal

Dye-based stains like red wine and fruit juice are treated with targeted spotting agents before they set permanently into fibers.

Pet Stain Elimination

Coffee & Grease Stains

Solvent-based pre-treatment dissolves oil and grease that water-based cleaners alone cannot break down.

Ink & Dye Removal

Specialized solvents lift ink and dye stains from carpet and upholstery without damaging the surrounding fiber color.

Odor Elimination Included

Every stain treatment includes deodorizing — so the smell disappears along with the visible stain.

Extends Fabric & Carpet Life

Removing stains promptly prevents acidic residues from breaking down fibers over time, protecting your investment.

Our Method

How DRX Gets It Done

Every stain gets diagnosed before it gets treated — the right method, applied the right way, for a result that lasts.

Book a Cleaning
1
Stain Identification & Testing

We identify the stain type — organic, oil-based, or dye — and test fabric or fiber compatibility before applying any treatment.

2
Targeted Pre-Treatment

Enzyme solutions for organic stains, solvents for grease and oil, or specialized agents for dye-based stains are applied directly to the affected area.

3
Dwell Time

Treatment is given time to break down the stain at the fiber level before extraction begins — rushing this step is the most common reason DIY stain removal fails.

4
Hot-Water Extraction

High-pressure hot water and powerful suction lift the loosened stain and residue completely from carpet, upholstery, or mattress fibers.

5
Spot Check & Reapplication

Stubborn or older stains may receive a second targeted application to maximize the result before we move on.

6
Deodorizing & Final Dry

A fabric-safe deodorizer eliminates any lingering odor. Carpets are typically dry within 6–8 hours.
