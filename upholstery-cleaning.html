Why Upholstery Needs Professional Cleaning

Your Sofa Holds More Than You Think

Upholstered furniture absorbs everything — sweat, skin cells, food particles, pet dander, dust mites, and spilled drinks — faster than almost any other surface in your home. In Florida's humid climate, this buildup creates the perfect environment for bacteria and allergens to thrive deep inside fabric fibers.

Professional upholstery cleaning reaches what surface wiping cannot. DRX technicians identify your fabric type, select the appropriate cleaning method, and use hot-water extraction or low-moisture techniques to thoroughly clean and sanitize every inch of your furniture without damaging delicate fibers or causing shrinkage.

We clean all common upholstery fabrics including microfiber, polyester, cotton, linen, velvet, chenille, and blended materials. Before any cleaning begins, we perform a fabric test to confirm the safest and most effective approach for your specific furniture.

The result is furniture that not only looks visibly cleaner but is genuinely sanitized — free of the bacteria, allergens, and odor-causing residues that accumulate over months of regular use. Our customers regularly report that their sofa looks better than it has in years and that pet and food odors they had given up on are completely gone.

Why Clean Your Upholstery

Benefits of Professional Upholstery Cleaning

Stain Removal

Food, wine, coffee, juice, and pet stains that seem permanent respond to our professional enzyme pre-treatment and extraction. Most fabric stains are fully eliminated.

Odor Elimination

Pet accidents, food spills, and years of body odor create persistent smells. Our enzyme-based treatment eliminates these at the molecular level — not just with a fragrance cover-up.

Allergen Reduction

Sofas harbor dust mites, pet dander, pollen, and mold spores. Professional cleaning removes these triggers, providing significant relief for allergy and asthma sufferers.

Extended Furniture Life

Embedded soil particles break down fabric fibers over time. Regular professional cleaning removes abrasive particles and extends the life of your furniture by years.

Restored Appearance

Colors look brighter, textures feel softer, and your furniture recovers its original inviting appearance. Professionally cleaned upholstery makes your whole living room look refreshed.

Safe for Your Family

All products are eco-safe, non-toxic, and approved for homes with children and pets. Once dry, your furniture is completely safe for all contact — including babies.

Our Method

How We Clean Your Upholstery

Every piece of furniture is different. Our process begins with a thorough assessment to determine the safest and most effective approach for your specific fabric.

Book a Cleaning
1

Fabric Assessment & Care Code Check

We identify your upholstery fabric and check the manufacturer's care code (W, S, WS, or X) to determine the correct cleaning method — water-based, solvent-based, or dry-only.

2

Pre-Vacuuming & Dry Soil Extraction

HEPA vacuuming removes pet hair, dust, dry crumbs, and loose debris from all surfaces and crevices before any wet cleaning begins.

3

Pre-Spot Treatment

Stained areas receive targeted enzyme or solvent pre-treatment based on the stain type and fabric compatibility. We allow dwell time to break down stain bonds before extraction.

4

Deep Extraction or Low-Moisture Cleaning

Depending on fabric type, we use hot-water extraction (for W-coded fabrics) or a low-moisture encapsulation method (for more delicate materials) to deep clean fibers without over-wetting.

5

Deodorizing & Fabric Grooming

We apply a fabric-safe deodorizer and groom the upholstery nap to promote even, faster drying and restore the fabric's natural texture.

6

Final Inspection

We inspect every surface with you before leaving. Any area that does not meet your expectations is re-treated on the spot — at no additional charge.
