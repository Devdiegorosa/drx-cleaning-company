Every Rug Type Needs a Different Approach

Area rugs are among the most varied floor coverings in any home — from machine-made synthetic rugs in family rooms to hand-knotted Persian and Oriental rugs that represent significant investments. One-size-fits-all cleaning destroys rugs. DRX Cleaning uses fiber-specific methods that protect your rug while achieving a genuine deep clean.

Before any cleaning begins, we assess your rug's fiber type (wool, nylon, polypropylene, cotton, jute, silk blends), construction method (hand-knotted, hand-tufted, flat-weave, machine-made), and dye stability. This assessment determines whether hot-water extraction, low-moisture encapsulation, or dry compound cleaning is most appropriate.

Florida homeowners use area rugs extensively — in living rooms, dining rooms, bedrooms, and on tile or hardwood floors throughout the home. These rugs accumulate Florida-specific soiling challenges: high humidity promotes rapid dust mite and mold growth in rug fibers; sand from beaches and lakes works deep into pile construction; year-round pet activity creates persistent odor and stain accumulation.

DRX provides on-site area rug cleaning throughout Central Florida. You do not need to transport heavy or valuable rugs anywhere — we bring our professional equipment to you and clean your rugs in place.

Why It Matters

Benefits of Professional Area Rug Cleaning

Fiber-Safe Methods

Wrong cleaning methods shrink wool, bleed colors, and damage pile. Our fiber assessment ensures every rug receives the safest and most effective method for its specific construction.

Complete Stain Removal

Pet accidents, red wine, food, and tracked-in soil respond to our targeted pre-treatment and extraction. We test stain type and fiber compatibility before treatment for optimal results.

Odor Elimination

Enzyme-based treatment eliminates pet urine, musty, and food odors at the molecular level — not masking with fragrance. Effective on all rug fiber types.

Dust Mite & Allergen Removal

Area rugs harbor dust mites and allergens as effectively as wall-to-wall carpet. Professional extraction removes these triggers significantly, benefiting allergy sufferers.

Extended Rug Life

Abrasive soil particles cut pile fibers over time. Regular professional cleaning removes these particles and significantly extends the life of both inexpensive and investment-grade rugs.

Restored Color & Texture

Professionally cleaned rugs display brighter colors, even pile, and a refreshed appearance. Patterned rugs especially benefit from deep cleaning that brings out design clarity.

Our Method

How DRX Cleans Your Area Rug

Every rug receives individual assessment. Our process is calibrated to each rug's specific fiber, construction, and condition.

Book a Cleaning
1
Fiber & Color Fastness Assessment

We identify fiber type, construction method, and test color fastness in a hidden area. This determines the correct cleaning method — water-based, low-moisture, or solvent — for each rug.

2
Dry Soil Extraction

HEPA vacuuming removes dry sand, dust, pet hair, and debris embedded in pile fibers before wet cleaning — a step that significantly improves final results and protects fiber integrity.

3
Pre-Spot Treatment

Stained areas receive targeted pre-treatment matched to stain type and fiber compatibility. Pet stains receive enzyme pre-treatment; food and beverage stains receive appropriate solvent or water-based agents.

4
Fiber-Appropriate Deep Cleaning

Synthetic rugs receive hot-water extraction. Natural fiber rugs (wool, jute, cotton) receive low-moisture cleaning to prevent shrinkage and dye bleed. Delicate silks and antique rugs receive dry compound or minimal-moisture treatment.

5
Deodorizing & Pile Grooming

Fabric-safe deodorizer is applied, and pile is groomed to promote even drying and restore natural texture direction.

6
Final Inspection

We inspect results with you before leaving. If any area does not meet your expectations, we re-treat at no additional charge.
