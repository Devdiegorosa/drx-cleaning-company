Commercial Carpet Cleaning

Commercial Carpet Cleaning Built Around Your Schedule

Commercial carpets face a level of foot traffic that residential cleaning schedules cannot adequately address. Office buildings, retail stores, restaurants, and hotels see hundreds or thousands of visitors per week — each depositing dirt, oils, bacteria, and outdoor contaminants into carpet fibers. Without regular professional cleaning, commercial carpets deteriorate quickly, affect indoor air quality, and send the wrong message to clients and employees.

DRX Cleaning delivers professional commercial carpet cleaning throughout Orlando and Central Florida with one priority: minimal disruption to your business. We offer flexible scheduling including evening appointments, weekend cleaning, and early-morning service before business hours begin.

Our truck-mounted hot-water extraction equipment handles high-volume commercial cleaning efficiently. We can address entire floor plans, specific traffic lanes, meeting rooms, lobbies, or any combination of areas based on your priorities and budget. We work with property managers, facility managers, office administrators, hotel housekeeping directors, and business owners directly.

Commercial carpet cleaning with DRX extends carpet life, improves indoor air quality for employees and customers, and maintains the professional appearance of your space. We provide flexible contracts for regular maintenance cleaning on a schedule that works for your business — weekly, monthly, or quarterly.

Why It Matters

Benefits of Professional Commercial Carpet Cleaning

Minimal Business Disruption

We schedule around your business hours — evenings, early mornings, or weekends. Your team and customers experience no interruption to normal operations.

Extended Carpet Life

Regular professional cleaning removes abrasive soil particles that degrade commercial carpet fibers. Proper maintenance can double the usable life of commercial carpet, reducing replacement costs significantly.

Healthier Work Environment

Commercial carpets trap bacteria, allergens, and pollutants that affect employee health and productivity. Professional cleaning significantly improves indoor air quality.

Professional First Impression

Clean, well-maintained carpet tells clients and visitors that your business is professional, organized, and detail-oriented. It sets the tone for every meeting and customer interaction.

Volume-Based Pricing

Commercial cleaning is priced by square footage with volume discounts for larger facilities or recurring contracts. We provide detailed, itemized quotes with no hidden fees.

Maintenance Contracts Available

We offer scheduled recurring commercial carpet cleaning on weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or quarterly schedules. Maintenance contracts include priority scheduling and consistent pricing.

Our Method

How DRX Gets It Done

Every step of our process is designed to maximize results while being completely safe for your home, your family, and your property.

Book a Cleaning
1
Site Assessment & Custom Plan

We visit your facility to assess total square footage, carpet types, traffic patterns, and schedule requirements. We develop a custom cleaning plan and provide a detailed written quote.

2
Pre-Scheduling Coordination

We coordinate with your facility manager or administrator to schedule cleaning at the optimal time — usually evenings, weekends, or before business opens.

3
Commercial Pre-Vacuuming

HEPA commercial vacuuming covers all areas before wet extraction — critical in high-traffic commercial environments where dry soil accumulation is extreme.

4
Pre-Treatment of High-Traffic Zones

Heavy traffic lanes, entry areas, and identified stain zones receive professional pre-treatment before extraction begins.

5
High-Volume Hot-Water Extraction

Our truck-mounted equipment delivers consistent, high-volume extraction across large commercial areas. We work systematically to cover your entire footprint efficiently.

6
Rapid-Dry Finishing

Commercial spaces benefit from our rapid-dry techniques and strong extraction — minimizing moisture levels for faster drying to get your space business-ready as quickly as possible.
